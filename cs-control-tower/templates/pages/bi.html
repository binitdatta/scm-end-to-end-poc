How the BI Layer Works

The Control Tower runs a background thread that subscribes to erp.# on RabbitMQ. Every event is:

  1. Stored in erp_events (raw append-only log)
  2. Used to update campaign_summaries (denormalised rollup)
  3. On pod-confirmed events: also updates delivery_tracking

This read-model lets the AI answer analytical questions instantly without hitting the operational MySQL databases.
