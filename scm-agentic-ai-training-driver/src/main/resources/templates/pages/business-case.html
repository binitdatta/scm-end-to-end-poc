Business Case

A Promotion Spans the Entire Supply Chain

A single business objective requires coordination across seven independent operational domains. This is exactly where an agentic orchestration layer can add value.

Human goal

Launch the promotion, source the merchandise, procure it, receive it, fulfill store demand, ship it, and confirm delivery.

1CRMCampaign
2VendorRFQ & quotes
3ProcurementPurchase Order
4WMS InboundReceive
5OMSStore order

7TMSPOD
Without orchestration

Humans coordinate across application boundaries

Users understand the overall objective, but each application exposes only a fragment of the business process. People bridge the gaps by navigating screens, interpreting status and deciding what should happen next.

With bounded agentic orchestration

The goal becomes an executable plan

The agent translates the objective into approved capabilities, executes them in sequence, observes results and pauses when policy requires human judgment.

Why this is a strong teaching scenario

It exposes the hard parts that toy agent demos often hide.

Multiple systems, distributed state, partial failure, business approvals, event timelines, independent data ownership and the need to prove what actually happened.
